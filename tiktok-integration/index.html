Optional creator connection

Media Empire OS + TikTok

Media Empire OS provides an optional TikTok integration for authorized creators who choose to connect their TikTok accounts.

Integration details

TikTok Login

Media Empire OS uses TikTok Login Kit to request creator authorization through TikTok's official OAuth flow. Media Empire OS does not ask users to provide their TikTok passwords directly.

Account Information

When authorized by the creator and permitted by TikTok, Media Empire OS may access limited TikTok account information required to identify the connected account and support enabled workflow functionality.

Video Draft Upload

For creators who explicitly authorize the required permission, Media Empire OS can prepare and upload creator-approved video content to TikTok using TikTok's Content Posting API. The initial Media Empire OS workflow sends supported videos to TikTok as drafts/inbox uploads so the creator can review and complete the publishing process within TikTok.

Permissions

Depending on the functionality enabled by the creator and the access approved by TikTok, requested permissions may include:

  • Basic account information
  • Authorized profile information
  • Authorized account statistics
  • Authorized video information
  • Uploading creator-approved videos as TikTok drafts

Not every permission is requested or collected for every creator. Available permissions depend on the enabled workflow and TikTok’s authorization and approval requirements.

Disconnecting TikTok

Creators may revoke Media Empire OS access through applicable TikTok account settings or by contacting Media Empire OS using the contact information published on this website.
