Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Media Empire OS handles information when creators use the website, workflows, and supported platform integrations.

Effective Date: September 21, 2026

Privacy commitments

Information Users Provide

We may collect information users choose to provide, such as a name, email address, support request, privacy or deletion request, and other information submitted through available forms or communications.

Connected Platform Information

When a user connects a supported third-party account, Media Empire OS may receive account information made available by that platform and authorized by the user. The exact information depends on the platform, enabled feature, granted permissions, and applicable platform rules.

OAuth Authorization Information

Media Empire OS may process OAuth authorization data, including account identifiers, authorization scope, access tokens, refresh tokens, token status, and expiration information. Access and refresh tokens are used only to provide authorized functionality and should be stored in protected server-side systems.

Media and Workflow Information

We may process creator-approved media, captions, metadata, publishing settings, workflow status, validation results, delivery records, and other information needed to prepare and coordinate supported media workflows.

Technical and Log Information

We may collect technical information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service, such as browser and device information, IP address, timestamps, request and error logs, authorization events, security events, and integration delivery results.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide requested features, associate connected accounts, authenticate authorization, prepare and validate media workflows, coordinate supported platform delivery, maintain security, investigate failures, respond to requests, enforce applicable terms, and comply with legal obligations.

TikTok Integration

When a user chooses to connect TikTok, Media Empire OS may receive information and permissions authorized by that user and made available by TikTok APIs. This may include identifiers, basic account/profile information, authorized video information, and authorization credentials required to provide the requested integration.

Access and refresh tokens are used only to provide authorized functionality and should be stored in protected server-side systems. Media Empire OS does not sell TikTok user data.

Users may revoke TikTok authorization and may contact Media Empire OS to request deletion of associated stored integration data, subject to legal and security retention requirements.

Data Sharing and Service Providers

Media Empire OS may share information with connected platforms at the user’s direction, and with service providers that support hosting, storage, security, monitoring, media processing, or other operational functions. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with an organizational transaction where lawful safeguards apply.

Media Empire OS does not sell personal information or TikTok user data.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing enabled functionality, maintaining security and audit records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. Retention periods may vary by data type, workflow state, and applicable requirement.

User Choices, Revocation, and Deletion

Users may choose not to connect supported platforms, may revoke platform authorization through applicable platform settings, and may stop using an integration. Users may contact Media Empire OS to request access, correction, or deletion of associated stored data. Requests may be subject to identity verification and legal, fraud-prevention, security, or recordkeeping requirements.

Security

Media Empire OS uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. No system is completely secure, and the security of information cannot be guaranteed.

International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user’s own, where data protection laws may differ. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for cross-border processing.

Children’s Privacy

Media Empire OS is not directed to children under the age required to independently consent to data processing in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If such information may have been provided, please contact us so it can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

Policy Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the service, integrations, legal requirements, or data practices. The updated effective date will be shown on this page. Material changes may be communicated through the service or another appropriate channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy, privacy rights, platform authorization, or data deletion requests may be submitted through the Media Empire OS Contact page.
